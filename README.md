互動式研究報告：疫情後消費者購買生鮮行為分析
Interactive Research Report: Post-Pandemic Consumer Behavior in Online Grocery Shopping
專案簡介 / Project Overview
這是一個互動式的單頁網頁應用程式，將我的 MBA 碩士論文《疫情後消費者購買生鮮行為之改變》的學術研究成果轉化為視覺化和易於理解的商業洞見。

本專案旨在展示我將複雜數據分析轉化為具體商業策略的能力，並提供一個直觀的介面，讓使用者（如招聘經理）可以輕鬆探索研究的關鍵發現。

This is an interactive single-page web application that visualizes the academic findings of my MBA thesis, "Consumer Behavior in Post-Pandemic Online Grocery Shopping," into easy-to-understand business insights.

The project aims to demonstrate my ability to translate complex data analysis into concrete business strategies and provides a user-friendly interface for employers to easily explore the key research findings.

核心洞見與商業意涵 / Key Insights & Business Implications
本研究基於對 400 份問卷的分析，得出以下關鍵發現：

滿意度與習慣是關鍵驅動力：研究證實，先前滿意度（Prior Satisfaction）與慣性（Habit）是影響消費者態度最重要的因素。這代表企業應專注於提供卓越的購物體驗，並透過會員計畫等方式培養顧客習慣。

態度決定行為：消費者對線上購物抱持的態度，是預測他們是否會持續使用的最強指標。

策略建議：本專案為企業提供了數據驅動的策略建議，幫助他們有效提升顧客滿意度、培養顧客忠誠度，並在後疫情時代的市場中保持競爭力。

Based on the analysis of 400 survey responses, this study reveals the following key findings:

Satisfaction & Habit are Key Drivers: The research confirms that Prior Satisfaction and Habit are the most significant factors influencing consumer attitudes. This implies businesses should focus on providing an excellent user experience and nurturing habits through loyalty programs.

Attitude Predicts Behavior: A consumer's attitude toward online shopping is the strongest predictor of their Continued Use of the service.

Strategic Recommendations: The project offers data-driven strategies for businesses to effectively boost customer satisfaction, build loyalty, and maintain a competitive edge in the post-pandemic market.
