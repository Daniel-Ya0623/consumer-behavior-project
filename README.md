Interactive Research Report: Post-Pandemic Consumer Behavior in Online Grocery Shopping
Project Overview
This is an interactive single-page web application that visualizes the academic findings of my MBA thesis, "Consumer Behavior in Post-Pandemic Online Grocery Shopping," into data-driven business insights.

The project demonstrates my ability to transform complex research data into actionable strategies, providing an intuitive interface for recruiters and managers to easily explore key findings and their commercial value.

Key Insights & Business Implications
Based on the statistical analysis of 400 survey responses, this study reveals the following key findings and their strategic implications:

Satisfaction & Habit are Key Drivers: The research confirms that Prior Satisfaction and Habit are the most significant factors influencing consumer attitudes. This implies that businesses should prioritize creating a superior user experience to build positive attitudes and cultivate customer habits through loyalty programs.

Attitude Predicts Behavior: A consumer's attitude toward online shopping is the strongest predictor of their Continued Use of the service. This insight allows managers to focus on attitude-building strategies for effective customer retention.

Data-Driven Strategy: The project offers data-driven strategies that empower businesses to effectively boost customer satisfaction, build loyalty, and maintain a competitive edge in the post-pandemic market.

Technologies Used
Data Analysis

Descriptive Statistics: Applied for summarizing and interpreting survey data.

Frontend Development

互動式研究報告：疫情後消費者購買生鮮行為分析
專案簡介
這是一個互動式的單頁網頁應用程式，將我的 MBA 碩士論文《疫情後消費者購買生鮮行為之改變》的學術研究成果轉化為數據驅動的商業洞見。

本專案旨在展示我將複雜研究數據轉化為具體策略的能力，並提供一個直觀的介面，讓使用者（如招聘經理）可以輕鬆探索關鍵發現及其商業價值。

核心洞見與商業意涵
本研究基於對 400 份問卷的統計分析，得出以下關鍵發現及其策略意涵：

滿意度與習慣是關鍵驅動力：研究證實，先前滿意度（Prior Satisfaction）與慣性（Habit）是影響消費者態度最重要的因素。這代表企業應優先提供卓越的使用者體驗來建立正面態度，並透過會員計畫等方式培養顧客習慣。

態度決定行為：消費者對線上購物抱持的態度，是預測他們是否會持續使用的最強指標。這個洞見能讓管理者專注於建立正向態度的策略，以實現有效的客戶留存。

數據驅動策略：本專案為企業提供了數據驅動的策略建議，幫助他們有效提升顧客滿意度、培養顧客忠誠度，並在後疫情時代的市場中保持競爭力。
